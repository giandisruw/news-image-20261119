## Stories used in the image

### 1. Iran widens Gulf attacks to Kuwait and Bahrain (LEAD)
- Wed 18 Nov: Iran widened its attacks on Gulf neighbours, targeting
  the US Ali Al Salem air base in Kuwait and launching several attacks
  on Bahrain, in apparent retaliation for US strikes the day before.
- First Iranian attack on Kuwait since September. Iran's army said it
  targeted US drone and missile installations at Ali Al Salem; Kuwait
  said its air defences were countering hostile drone attacks.
- Bahrain said it intercepted Iranian aerial attacks for the third time
  this week; it accused Tehran of a "systematic hostile approach".
- Tue 17 Nov: a US fighter jet struck a Revolutionary Guard missile
  launch site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, in retaliation
  for an Iranian attack on a US radar installation in Bahrain earlier
  that day. Context: a nine-month-old war; the US says it is seeking
  a diplomatic end.
- Sources: Reuters (18 Nov, Enas Alashray), AP via Military.com
  (18 Nov, David Rising & Samy Magdy).

### 2. Trump, Xi and Putin meet today in Shenzhen
- Thu 19 Nov: first-ever trilateral meeting of the three presidents,
  hosted by Xi Jinping in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the 33rd APEC
  Economic Leaders' Meeting (18–19 Nov). Confirmed by China's Foreign
  Ministry on Mon 16 Nov; the White House and Kremlin had confirmed
  earlier.
- Expected subjects: nuclear arms (New START lapsed in February),
  artificial intelligence, trade, and the wars in Ukraine and Iran.
  Trump arrived from Manila, where he attended the ASEAN summit and
  met President Marcos.
- Sources: The Hindu BusinessLine (16 Nov), Reuters via World of Now
  (18 Nov), Daily Caller (16 Nov), Asia Today (16 Nov).

### 3. Democrats pick Jeffries for speaker, 251–11
- Wed 18 Nov (US): House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries as their
  candidate for speaker in a closed-door secret ballot, 251 votes to
  11, after the party retook the House in the 3 Nov midterm elections.
  Reported 19 Nov 00:11 UTC.
- If elected, Jeffries would be the first Black speaker in US history.
  The formal vote is by the full House when the 120th Congress
  convenes on 3 January 2027; he needs 218 votes and Democrats hold at
  least 257 seats. Mike Johnson remains speaker until then.
- Sources: Reuters (18 Nov), CNN (18 Nov), USA Today (18 Nov), NPR
  via KNKX/WHRO (18 Nov).

### 4. GTA VI rolls out around the world
- Thu 19 Nov: Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks at midnight local time in
  each market, starting with New Zealand — 13 years after GTA V.
  PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; no PC version announced. Standard edition
  $79.99.
- Review embargo lifted 18 Nov: Metacritic score 99 from ~100–140
  critic reviews. Sensor Tower: ~15.9 million digital preorders worth
  ~$1.5bn as of Tue 17 Nov.
- Sources: Reuters via TBS News (18 Nov), Stocktwits (18 Nov),
  The Gamers Cut (18 Nov), El Paso Times (18 Nov), Inc. (18 Nov).

### 5. Israeli tanks cross the Yellow Line (Gaza)
- Wed 18 Nov: Israeli tanks advanced in central Gaza, rolling along
  the main Salahudeen road west of the Yellow Line — the first such
  advance across the boundary since the October 2025 ceasefire.
  The military said it was a targeted operation against Hamas tunnel
  infrastructure; Hamas called it a ceasefire violation and a
  dangerous escalation. Dozens of displaced Palestinians fled.
- Same day, the US-backed Board of Peace reported to the UN Security
  Council that Israel and Hamas are both failing to meet key ceasefire
  commitments.
- Sources: Reuters (18 Nov, Nidal al-Mughrabi & Mahmoud Issa),
  SRN News (18 Nov), The National via tafs.uz (18 Nov).

### 6. Cyclone Akvan nears India's east coast
- Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan over the Bay of Bengal, ~500 km
  southeast of Narsapur on Wed 18 Nov evening; moving north-
  northwest at ~9 km/h. IMD issued red alerts for coastal Andhra
  Pradesh for 19–20 Nov; schools shut in six Odisha districts
  19–21 Nov.
- Revised forecast: landfall between Machilipatnam and Puri around
  noon Fri 20 Nov as a severe cyclonic storm, winds 90–100 km/h
  gusting to 110 km/h (down from the earlier very-severe forecast
  of 120–130 km/h).
- Sources: India Meteorological Department bulletins (18 Nov),
  The Daily Jagran live blog (18 Nov), The Hindu live updates
  (19 Nov 01:27 IST), Sambad English (18 Nov).
